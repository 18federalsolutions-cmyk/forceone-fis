About ForceOne–FIS

A Strategic Joint Venture for Federal Missions

ForceOne-FIS brings together ForceOne, a Native Hawaiian Organization partner, and Federal Information Systems, Inc. (FIS), an experienced federal and defense IT provider.

Together, the JV offers responsive teams for complex, security-sensitive missions while supporting the Native Hawaiian community’s long-term economic opportunity.

Technology built around the mission.
